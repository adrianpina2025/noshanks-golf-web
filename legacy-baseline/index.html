WHY NO SHANKS

Group golf has outgrown the group text.

The organizer should not become an unpaid help desk, and players should not search hundreds of messages. No Shanks gives the whole group one clear place to plan, communicate, execute and remember.

One source of truth.

Dates, people, registration fees, tee times, pairings, games, logistics and updates stay attached to the same experience.

Information before questions.

Each person receives what matters, when it matters—from an invitation and weather update to the final standings.

Service people remember.

Operators and clubs can deliver a branded, coordinated experience that creates loyalty, referrals and repeat business.

The trip lives on.

Courses, pairings, scores, winnings, photos and recaps remain together long after everyone gets home.
