THE COMPLETE PLAN

Everything the group will ask about—already answered.

Once the trip is confirmed, No Shanks becomes the shared headquarters for the organizer and every player.

People

Invitations, confirmations, guests, profiles, handicaps, rooming, arrival details and permissions.

Golf

Courses, tee times, pairings, tees, formats, teams, rules, caddies, fees and practice plans.

Travel

Flights, transfers, accommodations, meeting points, luggage, weather and local transportation.

Experience

Dinners, entertainment, non-golf activities, recommendations, dress notes and daily timing.

Money

Expected budget, deposits, balances, payment processing, shared costs, game contributions and secured winnings.

Communication

Announcements, reminders, questions, changes, notifications, photos and group conversation.
