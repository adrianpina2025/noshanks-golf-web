THE ORGANIZER’S CHECKLIST

Every moving part has a place.

From the first invitation to the last result, the event record stays complete and current.

Invite & confirm

Player invitations, guests, waitlists, reminders, participation status, waivers and profile information.

Secure the golf

Club selection, tee-time blocks, deposits, release dates, tees, handicaps, pairings and caddie requirements.

Set the competition

Formats, teams, rules, scoring permissions, contribution factors, projected winnings and live leaderboards.

Build the day

Arrival, check-in, practice, welcome, meals, entertainment, awards, transportation and other activities.

Collect the money

Entry fees, deposits, balances, add-ons, shared costs, payment reminders and settlement tracking.

Publish & notify

Event page, schedule, reminders, live changes, weather, announcements and role-specific notifications.
