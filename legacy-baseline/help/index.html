GAMES

Scoring and winnings

Nassau, Skins, Match Play, Best Ball and Stableford are supported, with player handicaps applied to the configured game.
The organizer can allow individual score entry or assign a scorer, captain, operator or club team member. Scores update the active games and standings across participating phones.
Contribution factors show how a player affected a team result—for example, holes counted in Best Ball, points produced, holes won or other format-specific contributions.
Projected winnings reflect the current live position while the game is still moving. Secured winnings reflect outcomes already closed under the rules configured for the event.
Yes. Where money games are enabled and lawful, No Shanks can track the amounts, show each player’s position and process the resulting payments through the configured payment experience.

OPERATORS

Acquire, deliver and retain

Operators can capture direct interest, publish experiences and respond to qualified organizer requests with complete proposals that include the itinerary, services, pricing and terms.
Yes. Operational communication can reach the full participating group. Follow-up marketing, future offers and referral activity are managed through permission-based participant preferences.
Yes. Operators can present branded participant communications, itineraries, trip pages and customer-facing experiences while No Shanks powers the underlying workflow.
Yes. Operators can track and process group payments and present relevant ancillary services such as dining, logistics, club shipping, activities and other travel partnerships.

CLUBS

Groups, events and trips

No Shanks supports visiting groups, organizer-led events, member-member and member-guest tournaments, invitationals, pro-ams and club-facilitated destination trips.
The club can connect a group tee-time block to the organizer’s confirmed participants, commitment deadlines, deposits and payment status. That gives the club better visibility before valuable inventory is held or released.
Yes. Club white label can carry the club identity across event pages, participant communications, schedules, scoring, results and recaps.
Participant counts, names, guest details, handicaps, pairings, tee-time needs, payments, special requirements and schedule changes remain connected to the event instead of being rebuilt in separate spreadsheets and email chains.
Use the club demonstration form and select the hosted groups, tournaments, member events, tee-time workflows or club trips you want to improve.

PAYMENTS

Commitments and processing

Organizers, operators and clubs can request deposits, scheduled installments, event fees, trip balances, optional services and game-related settlements through the configured payment workflow.
Access follows the role and permissions of the experience. Organizers and authorized operator or club staff can see the status needed to run the group; each player sees their own required and completed payments.
Payments are handled through the integrated payment provider. No Shanks stores the transaction status and information needed to operate the experience, while sensitive card data is handled by the payment provider.

MEMORIES

Recaps and history

The permanent history can include the itinerary, courses, participants, pairings, scores, game results, winnings, player and team statistics, photos, reviews and the final recap.
Players, organizers and authorized operator or club staff can add photographs according to the permissions established for the experience.
