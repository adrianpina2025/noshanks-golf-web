Use the short description for directory listings and the long description for articles, partnerships and company profiles.

SHORT DESCRIPTION

No Shanks connects everyone around the golf experience.

No Shanks is a premium platform for planning, delivering, playing and reliving group golf trips and events.

LONG DESCRIPTION

One experience for organizers, players, operators and clubs.

No Shanks brings the complete group golf journey into one premium platform. Organizers can plan trips and events, invite participants, compare proposals, coordinate logistics, collect payments and run live games. Players receive schedules, pairings, weather, caddie information, statistics, standings, winnings, photographs and recaps. Operators and clubs use dedicated workflows, participant communication and white-label experiences to deliver stronger service, protect relationships and create repeat business.

PRODUCT FACTS

Four audiences. One connected experience.

No Shanks supports the work before, during and after a golf trip or event.

ORGANIZER

Plan and run it.

Trip and event setup, invitations, confirmations, proposals, logistics, payments, pairings, games, notifications and recap.

PLAYER

Show up and play.

Schedules, tee times, weather, caddies, score entry, live standings, statistics, winnings, photos and permanent history.

OPERATOR

Deliver and retain.

Lead capture, proposals, group coordination, payment processing, participant relationships, ancillary services and white label.

CLUB

Host at your standard.

Hosted groups, events, tournaments, club trips, tee-time blocks, operations sync, scoring, payments and white label.
