WORKING PRINCIPLES

Premium does not mean complicated.

The same principles that guide the product should guide the way the team works.

Care about the details.

Small inconsistencies become visible very quickly in a product built around a complex shared trip.

Build for real golf groups.

The product should reflect how people actually organize, travel and compete together.

Keep complexity behind the interface.

The organizer should not have to understand the machinery to benefit from it.

Protect the culture.

Golf trips are social experiences. The software should support that fact, not flatten it.
