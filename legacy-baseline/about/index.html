ABOUT NO SHANKS

Golf trips are memorable.
Organizing them usually isn’t.

No Shanks was built around a simple idea: the trip should feel as good before the first tee as it does once the group is on the course.

Built for the group

WHY WE EXIST

The group trip has outgrown the group chat.

Golf groups were planning complex trips and events across messages, spreadsheets, email threads, payment lists and scorecards. Operators were delivering premium service through fragmented channels. Clubs were hosting valuable groups without one reliable operating record.

No Shanks brings those pieces together without trying to make golf feel like software.

THE PRINCIPLE

Organization should disappear.

Good technology removes coordination from the foreground rather than giving the organizer another job.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Better trips. Better connections.

The product should support the reasons groups travel together instead of reducing the trip to logistics.

The trip is the product.

The rounds matter. So do the people, places, dinners, travel and stories that survive the scorecard.

Organization should disappear.

The interface should remove coordination from the foreground and give the organizer time back.

Competition should stay fun.

Handicaps, formats and live standings should make the round better—not require a mathematician in the cart.

Partners deserve good tools.

Operators and clubs should be able to serve the group without rebuilding the customer experience from scratch.
